Jacobian lens · J-space report

Layer anatomy of

Each layer l gets an averaged Jacobian J_l = E[∂h_final/∂h_l] that transports a residual into the final-layer basis, where the model's own unembedding decodes it. A contrast lens replaces that average with a signed one — mean(J | +1) − mean(J | −1) over labelled prompts — so it carries not what an activation is disposed to say, but where in depth two classes disagree about it. This report holds every lens fitted for this model, side by side.

Spectrum, layer by layer

Every row is one layer's singular values, normalised by its own σ₀ and shown on a log scale — so the row says how quickly that layer's transport falls off across directions. A row that stays dark far to the right spreads its work over many directions; one that fades immediately concentrates it in a few. Reading top to bottom gives the whole network's profile at once.

The same spectra, overlaid

The rows above drawn as curves, one per layer, shaded from shallow to deep. This is the view that shows the shape of the decay and how it migrates with depth; the heatmap is the one to read exact layers off.

One layer at a time

The bars are a histogram of that layer's whole singular spectrum in log bins — the bulk. The marks above it are the leading singular values kept exactly: where they stand clear of the bulk, the layer has a few outlier directions doing work the rest cannot, and those are the ones worth decoding.

J_l is square — , since it maps a residual to a residual — so it also has an eigen-spectrum, plotted at right. That one is genuinely complex: J_l is far from symmetric. The singular values on the left are real for any matrix, square or not, being the square roots of the eigenvalues of the positive semi-definite J_lᵀ J_l — their realness was never evidence of squareness.

How many directions each layer uses

Effective rank — the participation ratio of the spectrum, out of dimensions. One line per lens, so the contrast lens can be read directly against the transport lens it was built from.

Where the two classes diverge

Size of the difference relative to the positive-class lens, by depth. Read it against the split-half line — shards hold disjoint pairs, so two independent estimates of the same difference agreeing near zero means that layer is sampling noise, not contrast.

What the leading mode says

Top singular direction of J_diff per layer, decoded through the model's unembedding. u₁ is the final-basis direction the classes most disagree about; the two rows are its two signs, so + is what the positive class pushes toward and − what the negative class does. Sign assignment within a layer is meaningful; comparing signs across layers is not.

The same transport, read in an SAE feature basis

Composing the lens with the sparse-autoencoder frames at both ends gives M_l = W_enc,targetᵀ · J_l · W_dec,lᵀ — how much one unit of feature i at layer l moves the pre-activation of feature j at the target layer. It is the paper's readout with the SAE dictionary in place of W_U.

The lift adds no rank. M is but factors through J, so its rank cannot exceed d_model. The dictionary is an overcomplete frame, not a bigger space — what its spectrum measures is J in the SAE geometry. Layer similarity of the lens geometry

Linear CKA between the J-lens vectors (rows of W_U J_l) of every layer pair. For the transport lens, blocks along the diagonal are the paper's sensory → workspace → motor split; for a contrast lens they are stretches of depth that separate the classes the same way. What each J matrix does

Transport gain and identity alignment by depth. For the transport lens, gain falls and alignment rises — late layers approach a wide pass-through. A difference matrix transports nothing, so its identity alignment sits near zero by construction.

Where the lens finds the unspoken concept

Six prompt sets from the paper, read out through the transport lens. Each item hides an intermediate concept the prompt never states; the line is the fraction of those concepts sitting at lens rank ≤ k at that layer. A hump in the middle of the network is the workspace signature.
